The Rarity Promise

Nobody in the room wears what you wear

Fast fashion makes thousands of the same thing. We make very few, and we spread them out. Each design has a fixed edition in every city. Once it's claimed, it's closed.

I.

Capped per city

Every design has a hard limit in each city, sometimes as few as eight pieces. At the wedding, the office party or the café, the person next to you won't be wearing your outfit.

II.

Numbered pieces

Your garment carries its edition number, like Piece 07 / 25 · Hyderabad. It's stitched in, it's on your edition card, and it belongs to you alone.

III.

Never restocked

When a city's edition is complete, that design is retired there for good. No re-runs, no "back by popular demand". That's why it's worth something.

Fashion, engineered

A spec sheet for every stitch

Online, a garment is usually just a photo and a size chart. We show you how it's built and how it will feel before you commit. Every design comes with:

  • An anatomy view. Tap the pins on the garment to see why each detail exists, from a hidden pallu anchor to a boned neckline.
  • A full spec sheet. Fabric composition, weight in GSM, lining, closure, length, care and making time.
  • A feel profile. Comfort, drape, stretch, breathability and formality, each rated 1–5, so you know how it moves before you wear it.
  • Side-by-side compare. Shortlist up to three designs and compare them line by line, the way you'd compare two machines.

Good questions

Before you claim your piece

Why are prices hidden?

So you look at the design first, not a number. Tap Reveal price on any design to see it instantly. A few couture and bridal pieces are priced personally by our stylist, because the final price depends on your choices.

What does "limited per city" actually mean?

Each design has a fixed number of pieces for each city we serve. The counter on every design shows how many are still unclaimed where you live. When it reaches zero, that design is closed in your city for good, even if other cities still have pieces left.

How do I reserve a piece? Do I pay online?

Choose your colour, size and add-ons, then share your name, mobile number and address. That reserves your numbered piece right away. Our stylist calls you to confirm measurements, final price and payment before anything is cut.

Can I get it made to my measurements?

Yes. Most designs offer Made to measure. Pick it as your size and enter your bust, waist, hip and height. Our stylist will check them with you on the confirmation call.

How long does it take?

Every design shows its making time in the spec sheet, usually 6 to 18 days, and up to 45 days for bridal couture. Your piece is made for you once you confirm, not taken off a shelf.

Can I tell you what I think of a design?

Please do. Every design has a one-tap feedback panel: Love it, Like it or Not for me, with room for a note if you want. We read all of it, and it shapes what we design next.
